About Geometry Dash

Developer: RobTop Games

Release Date: 13 August 2013

Platforms: Android, iOS, Steam

Geometry Dash is a horizontal runner-style casual/arcade game developed and published by RobTop Games. It was released for $1.99 USD (now $2.99 USD) on 13 August 2013 for Android and iOS, and for $3.99 USD (now $4.99 USD) on 22 December 2014 for Steam. On mobile devices, a lite version of the game is available, along with free expansions, Geometry Dash Meltdown, Geometry Dash World and Geometry Dash SubZero, which include exclusive levels.
